# Systematic review methods fixture

## Eligibility and question
Comparative health-intervention evidence for the active contrast and affected population.

## Search strategy
Named databases, date ranges, controlled vocabulary, free-text terms, language restrictions, and supplementary searches are recorded in the fixture protocol.

## Study selection
Two reviewers independently screen records and resolve disagreements by consensus. A selection flow and exclusion reasons are retained.

## Risk of bias and synthesis
Eligible studies receive design-appropriate risk-of-bias assessment. Effect estimates are synthesized by outcome; heterogeneity, indirectness, imprecision, and publication bias are assessed.

This is a structured test artifact, not a real systematic review.
