# Outcome-level GRADE certainty fixture

For both critical health outcomes, randomized evidence starts at high certainty and is downgraded once for imprecision. Risk of bias, inconsistency, indirectness, and publication bias are not downgraded. No rating-up domain applies. Final certainty is moderate.

The calculation path, domain rationales, and final ratings are represented in the Canonical EtD Record. This is a test fixture only.
