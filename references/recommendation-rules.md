# Recommendation Rules

## Process state

Use `recommendation_formed` or `insufficient_basis`. Insufficient basis requires null direction and strength, unresolved uncertainties, and next actions.

## Recommendation semantics

For `formal_grade_etd_draft`, a formed recommendation has direction `for|against` and strength `strong|conditional`. Conditions must be operational and traceable.

For a non-formal Canonical Record, direction and strength may both be null when those semantics do not fit the decision. Still provide a clear statement and rationale. If either direction or strength is used, use both.

## Contrast meaning

The recommendation applies to the focal option relative to the active comparator. Do not generalize it to unassessed alternatives.

## Strong recommendations

Low certainty, important value variability, substantial harms, equity losses, infeasibility, or irreversible error require explicit exceptional justification and often favor a conditional recommendation or insufficient basis.

## Consistency

Structured judgments control validation. Natural-language heuristics are secondary. A passing validator is not a substitute for panel deliberation or methodological review.
